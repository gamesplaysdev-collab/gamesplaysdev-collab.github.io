Jarful Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Jarful is a puzzle game made by Games Plays Dev ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Jarful has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your level and sound setting are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

Jarful is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language
  • how you interact with ads

Google handles this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time with the shield button at the top of the game screen.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Permissions

The game only uses internet access, which it needs to load ads.

Children

Jarful is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
